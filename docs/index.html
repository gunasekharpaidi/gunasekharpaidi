About

Hello, I’m Guna Sekhar.

I’m Guna Sekhar, a graphic designer with over seven years of experience turning ideas into thoughtful visual communication. My approach combines design fundamentals, visual storytelling, brand consistency, and meticulous execution.

From building distinctive brand identities to developing campaigns and digital assets, I enjoy solving visual problems and creating design that feels purposeful, memorable, and relevant.

I believe great design is more than aesthetics. It’s a way to make brands easier to recognize, understand, and remember.

How I work

  • Clarity first. Every decision has to help someone understand the brand faster.
  • Systems, not one-offs. I design rules that keep a brand consistent long after launch.
  • Craft in the details. Kerning, colour, print specs and file prep all matter.

Creative process

Four stages, from brief to production-ready files.

A clear process keeps projects on time and makes feedback easy. You always know what stage we’re in and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the brand, objectives, audience, and brief.

    You getA brief summary, key questions answered and research highlights.

  2. 02

    Define

    Establish the creative direction, references, and design strategy.

    You getA moodboard, creative direction and an agreed design approach.

  3. 03

    Design

    Develop concepts, refine details, and build the visual system.

    You getDesign concepts, refinement rounds and the complete visual system.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Prepare polished final artwork and production-ready files.

    You getPrint- and web-ready files, organised assets and usage guidance.

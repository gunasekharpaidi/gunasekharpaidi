# Image prompts — Guna Sekhar portfolio

Generate these, then send them back **with the exact file names**.
I'll place the real designs (logos, labels, layouts) onto the blank surfaces.

## Rules for every image

1. **Paste the style line at the end of every prompt:**

   > Photorealistic commercial product photography, shot on a medium-format camera, soft natural shadows, realistic materials and textures, high detail, clean professional composition. All surfaces completely blank: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no labels, no watermark.

2. **Keep blank surfaces facing the camera** and not covered by hands, objects or reflections. The flatter and more front-on, the more realistic my design will look on it.
3. **Size:** use the ratio given (Midjourney: add `--ar 16:10 --style raw`, or `--ar 4:5`). Minimum 1600 px on the long side. JPG is fine.
4. If the result has random text on it, regenerate. Clean and empty is what we want.
